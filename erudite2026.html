Sejarah SGS

SGS History

Sejarah ringkas Sekolah Menengah Sains Tuanku Aishah Rohani, dahulu dikenali sebagai Science Girls School.

A concise history of Sekolah Menengah Sains Tuanku Aishah Rohani, formerly known as Science Girls School.

2013

SGS menerima kelulusan untuk beroperasi dan memulakan pentadbiran awal dengan staf perintis.

SGS received approval to operate and began its early administration with pioneer staff.

6 Jan 2014

Pn. Hjh. Azizah bt. Musa melapor diri sebagai pengetua pertama Sekolah Menengah Sains Perempuan Seremban.

Pn. Hjh. Azizah bt. Musa reported for duty as the first principal of Sekolah Menengah Sains Perempuan Seremban.

25 Feb 2014

SGS menyambut kohort pertama pelajar, menandakan permulaan perjalanan pendidikan yang bermakna.

SGS welcomed its first cohort of students, marking the beginning of a meaningful educational journey.

26 Mei 2017

Sekolah ditukar nama kepada Sekolah Menengah Sains Tuanku Aishah Rohani, sempena DYMM Tuanku Aishah Rohani.

The school was renamed Sekolah Menengah Sains Tuanku Aishah Rohani, in honour of DYMM Tuanku Aishah Rohani.
